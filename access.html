01Does joining the waitlist commit me to anything?

No. You only sign a contract after the technical call, once you've seen the survey plan and the fixed price.

02How much does an early‑access survey cost?

Crew and logistics at cost, typically $150–400k depending on area, depth and terrain. The quote is fixed before mobilisation.

03Which countries can you survey in?

Any country where we can get land access and ship equipment. Offshore we can work in up to 2 km of water, and on land up to 3 km of altitude.

04What if my site isn't selected?

We tell you why and keep you first in line for general availability. Often it's only a timing issue.

05Who owns the results?

You do. We publish anonymised validation figures only with your written consent.
