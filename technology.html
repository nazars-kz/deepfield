Ask an engineer ↗
01How deep can you actually see?

Survey‑grade detail to 5 km. Below that, the volume is constrained by regional tomography and is marked in the model at lower confidence.

02Does it work under water or in cities?

Offshore it works to 2 km of water, using seabed nodes. In cities we survey at night and filter out traffic noise, and resolution drops by about 20%.

03What permits does a survey need?

Usually only a land‑access agreement. There are no explosives, no drilling and no vehicles off existing roads, and we prepare the environmental note for you.

04What format is the data delivered in?

SEG‑Y and LAS for your existing software and a LAZ point volume. There is also a browser viewer for people who don't use geoscience tools.

05Who owns the survey data?

You do. We keep a copy to improve the inversion only if your contract allows it.
