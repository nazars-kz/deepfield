T03 · How it worksDiagram band + 3 calloutsPlan (mechanism)Знімає: «Як звук може показати руду?»
How it works

One pulse. Three readings. One volume.

Every rock type slows sound differently. We measure that delay at thousands of points and invert it into a map of what the ground is made of.

Diagram · cross-section 21:9Surface with source and geophone ring. Wavefronts going down, reflections coming back at each boundary. Ore lens in the third layer. Line art only.
01 · EmitA broadband source sends a controlled pulse, 0.5–240 Hz.
02 · Listen81 geophones record every echo from every layer boundary.
03 · ReconstructFull-waveform inversion turns arrival times into a classified point volume.

T08 · Technical FAQAccordion, 5 rowsObjection handlingЗнімає: технічні заперечення геологів і HSE
Questions from geologists

The hard questions, answered.

Missing one? Our survey engineers reply within one business day.

Ask an engineer
01How deep can you actually see?

Survey-grade detail to 5 km. Below that the volume is constrained by regional tomography and shown with lower confidence, clearly marked in the model.

02Does it work under water or in cities?

Offshore to 2 km water depth with seabed nodes. In cities we survey at night and filter traffic noise; resolution drops by about 20%.

03What permits does a survey need?

Usually a land-access agreement only. There are no explosives, no drilling and no vehicles off existing roads. We prepare the environmental note for you.

04What format is the data delivered in?

SEG‑Y and LAS for your existing software, a point volume in LAZ, and a browser viewer for people who don't run geoscience tools.

05Who owns the survey data?

You do. We keep a copy only to improve the inversion models, and only if you allow it in the contract.

Waitlist questions.

No. You only sign a contract after the technical call, once you've seen the survey plan and the fixed price.

02How much does an early-access survey cost?

Crew and logistics at cost, typically $150–400k depending on area, depth and terrain. The quote is fixed before mobilisation.

Any country where we can get land access and ship equipment. Offshore up to 2 km water depth; on land up to 3 km altitude.
